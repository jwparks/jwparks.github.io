JIWOONG PARK, Ph.D.

POSTDOCTORAL RESEARCHER  ·  NEUROSCIENCE  ·  UC BERKELEY

I am a cognitive computational neuroscientist studying complex decision making and hierarchical action planning in human daily life. I use video games as naturalistic life simulators, combined with fMRI and computational modeling to understand how the brain organizes goal-directed actions across multiple timescales.

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher working with Jack L. Gallant at the Gallant Lab, UC Berkeley. I completed my Ph.D. in Biomedical Engineering at Sungkyunkwan University with Won Mok Shim at the PECON Lab, and my B.Sc. in Physics and Chemistry at Hanyang University, Korea.
